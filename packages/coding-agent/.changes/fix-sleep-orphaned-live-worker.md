- Fixed recovery treating a worker `list`, subscription, or IPC timeout as proof of death. A live or identity-unknown worker now keeps its descriptor, kernels, tools, and transcripts untouched and is re-probed at no more than a 30-second cadence until a complete roster restores routing; only a fresh `gone` or `replaced` identity may authorize uncertain-operation cleanup or relaunch.
- Fixed orphan cleanup allowing legacy pid-only journal entries to signal a potentially reused process. Cleanup now requires a matching process start identity and rechecks it in the same synchronous operation that sends the signal.
